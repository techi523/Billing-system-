Surf Fast. Manage Easy.

Welcome to SurfBill, the industry-leading SaaS for intelligent Wi-Fi billing. Whether you are running a single neighborhood hotspot or a regional ISP, SurfBill provides the automation, security, and analytics you need to scale.

💡
Pro Tip: Use the interactive setup wizard on your main dashboard to track your configuration progress. Most tenants are live in under 15 minutes!

Tenant Registration

1 Navigate to SurfBill.com and click "Start Free Trial". Enter your business email, phone number, and desired subdomain (e.g., superisp).

2 Verify Identity: Check your email for a verification link. This step is critical to prevent fraud and secure your billing data.

3 Branding: Go to Settings > Branding to upload your logo and choose your brand's primary color. This will automatically update your Captive Portal UI.

Adding Your Network Hardware

SurfBill connects directly to your edge routers via API. We currently support MikroTik (RouterOS v6/v7) out of the box.

1 Router API: Ensure your MikroTik has the API service enabled (/ip service set api disabled=no).

2 Credentials: Enter the Public IP, Admin Username, and Password in the "Add Router" modal.

⚠️
Security Warning: We recommend creating a dedicated API-only user on your router with limited permissions for maximum security.

Creating Billing Packages

Packages allow you to define how much users pay for specific access limits.

1 Plan Type: Choose between "Hotspot" (Time/Data Based) or "ISP" (Subscription Based).

2 Limits: Set a time limit (e.g., 1 Hour) or data limit (e.g., 500MB). You can also cap the download/upload speeds to ensure fair usage.

3 Pricing: Assign a KES value. SurfBill handles the M-Pesa rounding automatically.

M-Pesa Daraja Integration

Automate your revenue collection with Lipa Na M-Pesa STK Push.

1 Credentials: Go to Finance > Settings and enter your Daraja Consumer Key, Secret, Shortcode, and Passkey.

2 Test Mode: Enable "Sandbox Mode" to run test transactions without real money. Switch to "Production" once verified.

3 Auto-Reconciliation: When a user pays, SurfBill listens for the M-Pesa callback and activates internet in < 3 seconds.

Voucher Management

For cash payments or resellers, generate unique access codes.

1 Select a package and specify the "Quantity" of vouchers to generate.

2 Export: Download the list as a CSV to print on physical cards or share via SMS.

3 Agents: Use the "Agent Portal" to allow third-party sellers to sell vouchers for a commission.

Managing ISP Subscribers

For Home Fiber or Fixed Wireless operations using PPPoE.

1 Register: Add the subscriber's name, phone number, and desired PPPoE username/password.

2 Billing Cycle: Set the subscription to "Monthly Prepaid". The system will calculate the expiry date automatically.

Automated Suspension

Every hour, SurfBill scans for expired accounts. If a payment is not confirmed by the expiry date, the account is automatically disabled on the MikroTik router.

Reminders: Subscribers receive an SMS reminder 2 days before expiry (requires SMS Gateway config).

How to Connect (End-User)

Your customers experience a frictionless connection flow.

1 Connect: Select your Wi-Fi network from the device list.

2 Select: The SurfBill portal slides up. Choose your plan.

3 Pay: Enter your M-Pesa number. Confirm the prompt on your phone.

4 Enjoy: You are online instantly. The portal displays your remaining time.

Advanced Intelligence

AI-Based Suggestions

SurfBill analyzes your sales data to suggest the best prices for your location. If users are dropping off on 1-Hour plans, the AI might suggest a "2-Hour Power User" bundle at a competitive rate.

Loyalty Rewards

Enable gamification to reward frequent users with bonus time (e.g., Use 5 times, get 1 Hour Free).
